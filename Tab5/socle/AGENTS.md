# AGENTS.md — `Tab5/socle/` (pure C++, tested on a PC)

Read with the root [`AGENTS.md`](../../AGENTS.md) and [`Tab5/AGENTS.md`](../AGENTS.md). Claude Code loads this file (through the `CLAUDE.md` next to it) only once it reads or edits a file of this folder.

`socle/` is pure C++, built and tested on a PC, no ESPHome nor LVGL (`tests/test_rangement.py`). Host tests: [`tools/AGENTS.md`](../../tools/AGENTS.md).

## Toolbox — pure helpers (`Tab5/socle/`)

Before writing a helper, look here and `grep` the name; when you add a shared helper of this folder, add its line here. The helpers of the LVGL layer are in [`Tab5/ecran/AGENTS.md`](../ecran/AGENTS.md), the games' in [`Tab5/jeux/AGENTS.md`](../jeux/AGENTS.md).

- **A number from Home Assistant to an int**: `tab5_float_vers_int(v, bas, haut, defaut)` (bounded, non-finite → default; a raw cast is undefined behaviour), `tab5_fini_ou_nan()` — `Tab5/socle/tab5_core.h`.
- **Brightness 0-255 → %**: `lum_pct()` — `Tab5/socle/tab5_core.h` (the only formula of the card, the light popup and the wheel).
- **Reading a payload from Home Assistant without copying it**: `Champ` {p, n}, `champ_suivant()`, `champs_decouper()` (the rest ignored past `max`) / `champs_decouper_reste()` (the last field takes the rest), `champ_est()`, `champ_nombre()` (empty, unreadable, non-finite or longer than 31 characters → default), `champ_entier()`, the common size cap `kPayloadMax` — `Tab5/socle/tab5_champs.h`.
- **Splitting a payload in place**: `split_fields()` (keeps empty fields, unlike `strtok_r`), `trim_ws()` — `Tab5/socle/tab5_core.h`.
- **Clock and dates**: `tab5_time_source` (never `time(nullptr)` directly), `tab5_heure_valide()` (the only "clock is set" threshold, `kHeureValideMin`), `jour_civil()`, `annee_bissextile()`, `jours_du_mois()`, `hhmm_minutes()` (strict « HH:MM »), `local_day_from_offset()`, `local_day_number_today()`, `cal_index_for_offset()`, `day_long_utf8()` / `month_long_utf8()` / `day_short_utf8()` / `month_short_utf8()`, `format_short_day_label()` / `format_long_day_label()`, `ha_day_name()` — `Tab5/socle/tab5_core.h`.
- **Screen language**: `tr()`, `tr_ctx()`, `tr_fill()`, `tr_noop()` — `Tab5/socle/tab5_i18n.h`. A title in capitals, accents included (« BATTERIE RÉSEAU »): `texte_majuscules()` — `Tab5/socle/tab5_core.h`.
- **Energy balance** (pure, tested on a PC, [ADR-0062](../../docs/decisions/0062-energy-balance-kwh-grid-battery.md)): `energie_bilan_lire()`, `energie_bilan_creneau()` (net = produced − consumed), `energie_cumul()`, `energie_mois()` (the calendar month and its projection), `energie_echelle()` (bars and running total on one zero), `energie_rond()` / `energie_pas()` (round scale and step), `energie_jour_plus()` — `Tab5/socle/tab5_parse.h`.
- **Geometry of the popups built in C++**: `kEcranL`/`kEcranH`, the modal card `kCarteL`/`kCarteH`/`kCorpsY`, the body of the card popups `kCorpsX`/`kCorpsW`/`kCorpsH`/`kCartesEcart`/`kGraphiqueL`/`kAxeLibelleL`, two half cards `kDemiL`/`kDemiX`, header tabs laid out for the Tab5 and brought to the card from the × `onglet_x()`/`onglet_echelle()`, calendar columns `kCalColX0`/`kCalColPas`/`kCalColW`, room rows `kPieceLignePas`/`kPieceLigneH`, centre card panels `kPanneauCentralL`, rooms `kPiecesMax`/`kPiecesDefaut` and weather pages `kPagesMeteo`, elements of a room `kTuilesMax` (20) on `kPlacesTuiles` (5) places — `Tab5/socle/tab5_geometrie.h`, all derived from the board's screen (ADR-0061; YAML tokens `corps_w`, `corps_h`, `graphique_l`, `demi_w`, `demi_x`, `onglets_fin`, `onglets_zone`, `cal_col_*`, `piece_ligne_*`, `central_w`, kept equal per board by `tests/test_geometrie_partagee.py`).
- **What room tiles and the − / + tile share** (namespace `modele_ha`): `kNom`/`kIcone`/`kEtat`, `est()`, `etat_indisponible()`, `copier_icone()`, and the location key of a tile `tuile_cle(r, t)` (« tRT ») / of a room `piece_cle(r)` (« pR ») — `Tab5/socle/tab5_modele_ha.h`. Their `Type`/`Def`/`Etat`/`Vue` share a name only: different content, and `Def`/`Etat` are stored as-is in NVS — don't merge them.
- **The screen's rest rotation** (a board mounted upside down, [ADR-0069](../../docs/decisions/0069-screen-direction-from-home-assistant.md)): `rotation_repos()` — what the boot bar and Neon Apron read, never `kRotationRepos` (the maker's mounting) — and `rotation_sens(repos, retourne)`, `Tab5/socle/tab5_geometrie.h` (a game that turns LVGL sets `rotation_tenue()` while it holds its own angle); set while running by `dalle_sens_choisir()` (`Tab5/ecran/tab5_dalle.h`), called by the 7B's « Sens de l'écran » select only.
- **How many rooms, and which page shows which room** (ADR-0066): `pieces_nombre_lire()` (« pn|N », 1 … 10, 5 by default), `piece_de_page()` / `page_de_piece()` (weather page ↔ room; pages 5 and more = the room), `meteo_page_suivante()` (the weather swipe, rooms after days 10-14), `page_non_vide_proche()`, `pages_repartir()` (N items into pages of at most M, evenly) — `Tab5/socle/tab5_core.h`; a room digit `0` … `9`: `modele_ha::piece_index()`; the live count: `tuiles_pieces_nombre()` / `tuiles_pages_meteo()` — `Tab5/ecran/tab5_internal.h`.
- **Pure logic tested on a PC**: new logic without LVGL goes next to `Tab5/socle/tab5_core.*`, `Tab5/socle/tab5_champs.*`, `Tab5/socle/alarm_clock.*`, `Tab5/socle/tab5_economie.h` or `Tab5/socle/tab5_batterie.h`, tested by `tools/test_alarm_clock.cpp` or `tools/test_tab5_socle.cpp` (g++ in CI). A new reading of a payload pushed by HA goes into `Tab5/socle/tab5_parse.*` (lot F), with its cases in `tools/test_parse.cpp` and a call from the libFuzzer harness `tools/fuzz/fuzz_parse.cpp` (`tests/test_fuzz_parse.py` fails otherwise).
