# ADR-0055: Fridges and freezers — levels decided by Home Assistant against food-safety norms, a « Froid » popup, central-card alerts and a blinking icon on the clock, pushed by `tab5_maj_froid`

**Status:** Accepted (2026-10-10, asked for by the author; not tried on a tablet when written).
**Date:** 2026-10-10

## Context

The author wants to watch his fridge and freezer from the tablet: declare them in the settings, check their temperature against the norms, catch a rise (a door left ajar), see the last 24 hours, get the usual alerts of the central card, and — for a serious problem only — an icon that blinks in the clock's top-right corner. A full-screen popup gathers the curves and the incidents.

Constraints: push-only and events-only ([ADR-0001](0001-push-only-zero-polling.md), [ADR-0025](0025-events-only.md)); no entity ID in the firmware or in the public HA files ([ADR-0024](0024-packages-without-placeholders.md)); colours from the palette ([ADR-0029](0029-themes-palette.md)); `lv_chart` is not compiled; instant transitions, no fade (author's taste).

## Decision

- **Declared in Home Assistant, never guessed.** Two multiple-choice lists built like « Tab5 · capteurs suivis » ([ADR-0054](0054-tracked-sensors.md)): « Tab5 · réfrigérateurs · fridges » (`select.tab5_refrigerateurs`) and « Tab5 · congélateurs · freezers » (`select.tab5_congelateurs`), memories `input_text.tab5_choix_frigos` / `tab5_choix_congelateurs`. Only temperature sensors with statistics are offered (`device_class: temperature`, `state_class: measurement`); a sensor chosen in one list is not offered in the other; **four appliances at most in all** (the popup's four cards). Empty lists work: the popup says where to declare them.
- **Home Assistant decides, the tablet shows.** `packages/tab5_froid.yaml` computes, for each appliance, a level (0 within norm, 1 attention, 2 serious), a cause (`ok`, `chaud`, `froid`, `porte`, `indispo`) and since when, from the current value and `recorder.get_statistics` (5-minute statistics for the last 30 minutes, hourly ones for the curve and the 24-hour extremes). The thresholds are in **one place**, the `seuils` variable of `script.tab5_froid_calculer`, commented:
  - fridge: norm 0 to 5 °C; attention when the 15-minute mean is above 5 or below 0; serious above 8 for 30 minutes, or at 10 or more;
  - freezer: norm -18 °C at most; attention when the 15-minute mean is above -15; serious above -12 for 30 minutes, or at -10 or more;
  - door: a rise of 2 °C (fridge) / 4 °C (freezer) between the lowest of the last 30 minutes and now is « porte ouverte ? » (attention); still there after 20 minutes **and not going down** (no more than 0.5 °C under the episode's highest) it is « porte mal fermée » (serious). The « not going down » guard is ours: a door closed after a long opening cools down slowly and must not end as « mal fermée »;
  - no reading for 30 minutes: « capteur muet » (attention); shorter gaps keep the previous state.
  A Fahrenheit sensor is converted. The rules are applied in that order (first true wins) and mirrored by an independent Python calculation in `tests/test_froid.py`.
- **State kept in Home Assistant, not in the tablet.** A trigger-based template sensor `sensor.tab5_froid` (state = the most serious level, attribute `appareils`, restored at restart) is updated only by the `tab5_froid_bilan` event that the script fires; the script reads its previous attributes (when a cause started, the running incident, the last finished one). An incident runs from the first non-zero level to the return to 0; the last finished one (most serious cause, start, duration, extreme) stays with the appliance.
- **When.** A mirror (« Tab5 · signature du froid ») changes when a sensor changes: an automation in mode `single` computes at once, waits one minute (changes meanwhile are dropped) and computes again if the signature moved. A second automation computes every five minutes (durations move on without a change), when a list changes, when the tablet connects, on « MAJ Écran » and at HA start (`tab5_origine` guard). The state is kept even when the tablet is away; the push needs `tab5_connectee`.
- **One new action, one compact payload.** `tab5_maj_froid(payload)`: one record per appliance, fifteen `|` fields — `name|type|value|level|cause|since|min|max|low|high|points|icause|istart|iduration|imax` — temperatures in °C with one decimal (empty: unknown; empty low: no lower limit), the norm pushed by HA (the tablet hard-codes none), 24 hourly means then the current value. Read in pure C++ by `froid_lire()` (`Tab5/socle/tab5_parse.*`, section 12; cases in `tools/test_parse.cpp`, called by the libFuzzer harness and fuzzed with the other services): four appliances, 32 points, temperatures bounded to ±80 °C, an unknown type skips the record, a level above 2 is 2.
- **Alerts of the central card.** A source « froid » in `custom_templates/tab5_alertes.jinja`: one stable id per appliance (`froid:<entity_id>`), revision key = cause and level (a value that changes only updates the text), Orange at level 1, Red at level 2, coded text `@froid:<cause>:<level>:<value>:<name>` composed by the tablet in its language (`ha_alerte_texte()`: « Frigo cuisine : porte mal fermée, 9.1 °C »). Subscription: « Tab5 · alertes : réfrigérateurs et congélateurs » (Oui / Non). Doubt (`sensor.tab5_froid` unavailable) is never an end.
- **The blinking icon.** `fridge-alert` (F11B1, `mdi_font_26`, role `ERROR`) in the clock tile's top-right corner (`btn_froid_alerte`, the 27 px left of the minutes' frame), shown only while an appliance is at level 2. It blinks without fade: one `lv_timer` of 500 ms created once, paused while the icon is hidden; it stays lit while the Froid popup is open. No acknowledgement: it stays as long as the serious level lasts. A tap opens the popup through `tab5_ecran_ouvrir`. It follows the theme (role style).
- **The « Froid » popup.** Full-screen modal with the shared chrome ([ADR-0009](0009-modal-shell-header.md)), one `ModalRegistry::add` line ([ADR-0013](0013-single-registry-consoles-modals.md), 26 of 32), `Ecran::FROID` (option « Froid » at the end of « Aller à l'écran », gesture code `froid` at the end of `kCodesGestes`, index 27, offered by the blueprint), and in the navigation wheel ([ADR-0042](0042-navigation-wheel.md)) at the end of **Agenda** — Devices (8 of 8) and the first ring (6 of 6) are full; offered unless Home Assistant said no appliance is declared. Its title is « Frigos et congélateurs » and its wheel word « Frigos »: the key « Froid » already translates as « Cool » (a climate mode, a lamp's white). One card per appliance (`froid_carte.yaml`, four instances), one row for one or two, two rows of two above: icon (fridge or snowflake), name, temperature coloured by level (`SUCCESS` / `WARNING` / `ERROR`), status (« Conforme », « Trop chaud depuis 14 h 32 », « Porte mal fermée »…), 24-hour extremes and the norm, the 24-hour curve (`ui_courbe_lisse()`) between the dashed lines of the norm, and the last incident.

## Consequences

- Contract stays 1.2.0: one action added since the last release (v3.8.0-rc.4, contract 1.1.0), already a minor step. Update order: **firmware first**, then `packages/tab5_reglages.yaml`, `packages/tab5_froid.yaml`, `packages/tab5_alerts.yaml`, `custom_templates/tab5_alertes.jinja` and the blueprint. New HA files with an older firmware: the push fails without stopping anything else (`continue_on_error`), and the alert text shows raw (`@froid:…`) until the firmware is updated; new firmware with older HA files: the popup says « En attente de Home Assistant ».
- The 5-minute statistics are compiled after their period ends: the current value always joins the 15-minute mean and the 30-minute minimum, so a rise is seen at once.
- A door opened for shopping can raise « porte ouverte ? » (attention, Orange) for a few minutes: that is the requested threshold. It becomes serious only if the rise lasts 20 minutes without cooling down.
- 37 new screen texts in six languages; MDI glyphs `fridge` (F0290: 26, 32, 36, 45, 70) and `fridge-alert` (F11B1: 26).
- Render: three captures (`froid-vide`, `froid`, and `accueil-froid`, not compared: the icon blinks).
