# ADR-0077: The « Charges » page of the Energy popup

**Status:** Accepted (2026-11-09).
**Date:** 2026-11-09

## Context

[ADR-0074](0074-car-charges-analysis.md) lets Home Assistant work out, from the recorder, the list of the last charges of each car, the share of the sun in each and the month's balance. [ADR-0076](0076-cars-on-the-flux-page.md) shows the cars on the Flux page. Nothing shows the list yet: lot 3 of the author's plan for the cars (sketch B3) is a fifth page of the Energy popup, « Charges ».

## Decision

- **One service, the output of the analysis pushed as it is.** `tab5_maj_voiture_charges(payload)`: up to **six lines separated by `;`**, the most recent first, each `rang|jour|heure|duree_min|kwh|part_soleil_pct` — exactly the `charges` string that `script.tab5_voitures_analyse` returns ([ADR-0074](0074-car-charges-analysis.md)), so Home Assistant converts nothing. `rang` is 1 or 2 (the car of `tab5_maj_voiture`), `jour` is `YYYY-MM-DD` and `heure` `HH:MM`, local time, of the start; `duree_min` in minutes, `kwh` received by the car, `part_soleil_pct` from 0 to 100. A field left empty is unknown (a charge with no solar sensor chosen has an empty share: « -- », no bar); an **empty payload clears the list** and the page disappears. The reading is `charges_lire()` (`Tab5/socle/tab5_parse.*`, pure, tolerant like `voiture_lire()`: a line with no rank 1 or 2, or with nothing readable at all, is skipped; an unreadable date or hour only leaves them blank; more than six lines are cut; tested on a PC and fuzzed). The total and the sun's share of the total are `charges_total()`, weighted by the kWh. The contract goes to **1.6.0** (a pure addition); the firmware must be updated first.
- **The plan's format was right** — it matched the real output of the script, so nothing was corrected there. What the plan did not say: nothing in a line tells that a charge is **still running**. The page takes the most recent line of a car for the running one when `tab5_maj_voiture` says that car is charging (green bolt instead of the car icon, green duration, a light band behind the row, « en cours » in the legend). An older line is never shown as running.
- **The fifth page.** `kEnergiePages` is 5; the page sits between « Production » and « Bilan » (`P_CHARGES = 3`), has its container, its tab and its case in `peindre()`, and **exists only once the tablet has received a charge** (`page_a_donnees`): with no car configured the popup keeps its four tabs of 200 px and every existing screen is the same pixel for pixel. With the fifth page the five tabs are 158 px (`pages_onglet_largeur`, [ADR-0046](0046-popups-a-pages.md)); the names « Aujourd'hui », « Production » and « Charges » are checked at that width in the seven languages (the German tab is « Laden »).
- **The drawing** is built once at the first opening from the bricks that exist (`ui_rectangle`, `ui_text`, `ui_poser`; colours replayed on a theme change, `UIColor` roles only): a title and a legend, six rows of a car icon, the car's name, the day with its hour (« Aujourd'hui · 09:02 », « Hier », then « Dim 8 »), the duration, the kWh, a bar sun / grid and the share in %, and under the last row a total (« Total : 95,1 kWh, dont 62 % par le soleil »). Every text is 22 px or more. The bar is the sun in front of the grid on a track: **not on the 7B** (946 px against 1202, the day column takes its room and the percentage says the same).
- **Home Assistant side.** `script.tab5_energie` calls `script.tab5_voitures_analyse` with the sensors of the blueprint's « Énergie : voitures » section (the charge power of each car, renamed `puissance_charge`, plus the solar sensors, the home and the « meter separate from the cars » option) and pushes the result with `continue_on_error`. The analysis reads thousands of statistics rows: **at the first turn (the popup opens), then at most every 5 minutes** (`charges_t`), never at each turn of the loop, and not replayed on a change of view. A car with no power sensor is not analysed; with no such car, or without the analysis script, nothing is computed or pushed; a failed analysis pushes nothing (the tablet keeps its last charges). No entity ID in the public files ([ADR-0024](0024-packages-without-placeholders.md)).

## Consequences

- No car chosen: nothing is pushed, the tab is not there, the screens are those of before (proved by the off-device render compared byte for byte, and by the geometry tests).
- With both cars removed from the blueprint after charges were received, Home Assistant pushes nothing and the tablet keeps the old list until its next restart (same limit as the cars of ADR-0076; the `Charges` page is the only trace).
- The one-hour resolution of the old statistics (ADR-0074) bounds how far back the six lines go: a car that charges rarely may show fewer than six, or older ones than a week.
- About 70 more LVGL objects are created when the popup is first built (six rows of ten, a title, a legend and a total); the RAM and PSRAM were not measured for this change.
- **Not judged on the real screen**: the fit of the longest day names in the narrow 7B column and the weight of the bar. The off-device render shows both; the author's eye on the tablet decides.
