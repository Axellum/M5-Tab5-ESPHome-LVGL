# ADR-0061: The board and the screen size are a compile-time choice (`tab5_carte`), one package per board, the size written once

**Status:** Accepted (2026-10-12, lot 1 of the Waveshare 1024×600 port; the Waveshare board compiles but has never run on a board when written).
**Date:** 2026-10-12

## Context

Axel ordered a Waveshare ESP32-P4-WIFI6-Touch-LCD-7B (1024×600, landscape panel, ESP32-P4 revision 3, 32 MB flash). The firmware assumed one board everywhere: the Tab5's hardware in `tab5-hardware.yaml` and its screen revision in `ecran-*.yaml`, `1280`/`720` written in the tokens, in `tab5_geometrie.h` and in the C++ that derives from it, `rotation: 270` in LVGL and in the pinball's return to landscape, the font sizes in pixels.

The goal of this first lot: make the board and its screen size a parameter **without changing a pixel or a behaviour on the Tab5**, and have the Waveshare firmware compile completely. Adapting the interface to 1024×600 is a later lot.

## Decision

- **One substitution, `tab5_carte`** (`Tab5/user_entities.yaml`; `tab5` by default through Jinja, like `tab5_ecran`), picks `Tab5/paquets/carte-<carte>.yaml` at the place `tab5_hardware` and `tab5_ecran` had in the `packages:` block. A board package carries the hardware **and** the geometry:
    - `carte-tab5.yaml` loads `geometrie-tab5.yaml`, `tab5-hardware.yaml` and the screen revision `ecran-*.yaml`, unchanged;
    - `carte-waveshare-7b.yaml` holds the whole hardware of the Waveshare (pins from the compiled trial `essais/waveshare-p4-7b.yaml` of the `feat/essais-ecrans-waveshare` branch) and loads `geometrie-waveshare-7b.yaml`.
- **The geometry of a board is six substitutions** in `geometrie-<carte>.yaml`: screen size in landscape (`carte_ecran_l`, `carte_ecran_h`), panel size as the display driver sees it (`carte_dalle_l`, `carte_dalle_h`), LVGL rotation (`carte_rotation`: 270 on the Tab5, whose panel is portrait; 0 on the Waveshare when written, 180 since the board arrived, see [On the board](#on-the-board-2026-10-14)) and the scale of the large fonts (`carte_police_echelle`: 1, and 0.82 on the Waveshare, a little under 600 / 720 ≈ 0.83).
- **The size is written once.** The tokens derive from it (`modal_card_w: ${ carte_ecran_l | int - 30 }`, `cal_grid_h` from `modal_card_h`); the C++ gets it through `-DTAB5_ECRAN_L`, `-DTAB5_ECRAN_H`, `-DTAB5_ROTATION` build flags in both configurations (`tab5-ha-hmi.yaml`, `tab5-rendu-host.yaml`), with the Tab5's values as defaults in `tab5_geometrie.h` for the PC tests; `kCarteL`/`kCarteH` derive from `kEcranL`/`kEcranH`, and the pinball returns to `kRotationRepos`. `tests/test_geometrie_partagee.py` checks the C++ against the tokens **for every board**. The layout literals (positions and sizes of the widgets) are deliberately left alone.
- **Large fonts follow the board** (`roboto_32_b`, `roboto_45_b`, `roboto_55_b`, `roboto_130_b` in `tab5-styles.yaml`, and the theme fonts generated by `tools/gen_themes.py`): `size: ${ (N * (carte_police_echelle | float)) | round | int }`, which gives `N` on the Tab5.
- **The hardware the Waveshare lacks keeps its ids, as stubs.** The other packages (diagnostics, battery, energy saving, settings) reference the Tab5's power switches, battery gauge, headphone detect and RTC. Rather than make each of them conditional, `carte-waveshare-7b.yaml` turns them into template entities with `!extend`/`!remove` (no pin, optimistic switches, `update_interval: never` sensors), removes the RTC and its 24 h interval (which gets an `id`, `tab5_rtc_demarrage`, for that) and keeps the time-zone restore. The IMU is chosen in the root file: `tab5-imu.yaml` on the Tab5, `tab5-imu-absente.yaml` (the « Tap-to-Wake » switch only, without a name) elsewhere. The least intrusive option: the Tab5's packages do not change. The price: the blocks copied from `tab5-hardware.yaml` into the Waveshare package (logger, OTA, backlight output, media player…) can drift; `tests/test_carte_7b.py` keeps them equal.
- **The off-device render follows `tab5_carte`** (`geometrie-<carte>.yaml` in `tab5-rendu-host.yaml`, snapshot panel at the board's size). `rendu-host.yml` gets a second job, `rendu-7b`, which renders every screen at 1024×600 into a separate artifact (`rendu-1024x600`), informative only: the « identical » comparison stays the Tab5's, and the scripts keep the Tab5's coordinates.
- **CI compiles the Waveshare** (`build-carte-7b` in `esphome-tab5.yml`, same triggers as `build`, not a required check).

## Consequences

- On the Tab5 the resolved configuration is the same as before, except for the three build flags (equal to the header's defaults) and the `id` of the RTC interval — checked by comparing the configurations resolved by `esphome config` before and after, substitutions aside. The `config_hash` changes anyway: ESPHome hashes the substitutions block, which gains the `carte_*` keys.
- The project name (`axellum.tab5-ha-hmi`), `esphome: name` and `friendly_name` do not change with the board: a Waveshare compiled with the defaults would announce itself as a Tab5.
- The Waveshare's interface is not adapted: popups and the home screen overflow at 1024×600 until a later lot replaces the layout literals. A compile-time layout check that only holds at the Tab5's size (the five rows of the House popup fit in the card, `tab5_maison.cpp`) is guarded by `kMiseEnPageTab5` (`tab5_geometrie.h`, true on the Tab5): it stays strict there and lets the Waveshare compile.
- Not verified when written: nothing ran on a Waveshare (display, touch, audio, Wi-Fi co-processor); the pins come from a trial configuration that was compiled, never run.
- The Waveshare needs ESPHome 2026.9.1 or later (2026.9.0 aborts while starting the display of a P4 v3, esphome#18984; found on the board on 2026-10-14). A `min_version:` in the board package would be overridden by the root file's (2026.9.0, the Tab5's floor, read as is by CI) when packages are merged, so the board package lists `Tab5/ecran/carte_7b_version.h`, whose `static_assert` on `ESPHOME_VERSION_CODE` stops the build of the Waveshare under 2026.9.1.

## Second lot: what the popups share follows the card (2026-10-12)

- **What every popup shares derives from the modal card**, in `tab5_geometrie.h` and as tokens of `tab5-ui-tokens.yaml`, kept equal per board by `tests/test_geometrie_partagee.py`: the body (`kCorpsW`/`kCorpsH`, `corps_w`/`corps_h`), the graph area of a full-width card (`kGraphiqueL`), two half cards side by side (`kDemiL`/`kDemiX`), the end of the header tabs (`kOngletsFin`), the calendar columns (`kCalCol*`), the rows of a room in the Lights and Shutters popups (`kPieceLigne*`, at most the Tab5's 92-pixel pitch), the centre card's panels (`kPanneauCentralL`). The House popup's rows share the height left under their headers (104 pixels at most, set by `disposer()`), and its compile-time check now holds on every board.
- **Header tabs keep the Tab5's layout and are scaled from the ×**: places and widths written for the Tab5 (x 318…1148, 200 pixels; the Alarm clock from 196) are brought to a narrower card by the ratio of the two zones, measured from the × (`onglet_x()`, `onglet_echelle()`; YAML `onglets_fin`, `onglets_zone`). The ratio is 1 on the Tab5, so nothing moves there.
- **Each popup's own layout is not touched** (positions inside its cards, heights of its rows, the Music card): later lots. Neither are the home screen and the games. Two exceptions, where the smaller card made two elements overlap on the 7B: the alarm clock's two rollers are placed from the middle of their card (the « : » is centred), and the climate's room temperature keeps its Tab5 height (below its card on the 7B, as in the first lot, instead of over the − / + buttons).
- **The off-device render measures instead of guessing**: every capture publishes the screen shown (the « Écran courant » name) and the number of drawn objects that LVGL clips (`Tab5/rendu/rendu_mesure.h`, render only); `bilan-7b` compares the 7B to the Tab5 (screens opened / expected, clipped objects per popup) in its job summary. The render robot replays the Tab5's gestures brought to the board's screen and opens through navigation what the 7B's home screen hides (`tools/rendu/vers_carte.py`, render action `rendu_ecran`); on the Tab5 the steps are unchanged.
- On the Tab5 the resolved configuration is identical to the first lot's (`esphome config`, substitutions aside).

## Third lot: the home screen is the Tab5's, brought to the card's screen (2026-10-13)

- **One rule for the whole home screen**: every position and size the Tab5 draws at 1280×720 is brought to the card's screen, `x × L / 1280` and `y × H / 720`, rounded to the nearest pixel in integers (`(v × L + 640) // 1280`). Same proportions, same grid, smaller. Each value is a token of `tab5-ui-tokens.yaml` (`acc_*` for the grid, the top buttons, the left zone, the « Ok Nabu » frame, the row under the clock, the − / + tile, the centre card, the bottom tiles and the HA-mode cards; `hor_*` for the clock), never a literal in the widget. The C++ that places home-screen objects uses the same formula (`acc_x()`, `acc_y()` and the `kAcc…` constants of `tab5_geometrie.h`: the centre card, the row's width, the HA-mode cards, the low anchor of a climate's wheel, the compact player). `tests/test_geometrie_partagee.py` checks every token against the C++ for every board, checks that each token gives back the Tab5's old value (`test_accueil_du_tab5_au_pixel`), and checks the grid's invariants on every board (columns and clock side by side within the screen, five tiles centred, the centre card between the frame and the tiles).
- **The clock follows its font, not the screen**: its digits use `roboto_130_b`, already scaled by `carte_police_echelle` in the first lot. Its inner layout (digit frames, « : », date, the cut between the three touch zones) is the Tab5's scaled by the same factor (`police_pct`, `-DTAB5_POLICE_PCT`, `police_px()`), then centred in the clock tile (`hor_dx`, `hor_dy`). `theme_polices()` places every theme's rollers with the same rule; on the Tab5 the factor is 100 % and the offsets 0. Values are scaled from the Tab5's rounded pixels, not recomputed from the font's units: on another board a frame can be 1 pixel wider than a digit's advance (`tests/test_horloge.py` allows 1 pixel there, and still requires the ink inside its frame).
- **Fonts**: `mdi_font_70` (top buttons, HA-mode cards, plant row) and `mdi_font_alert` (weather warnings in the tiles) follow `carte_police_echelle` like the text fonts. `mdi_font_45`, `mdi_font_56` (shared with the games' selector) and the weather fonts keep their size. Amended on 2026-10-18: the icons of the two temperatures above the − / + tile and the − / + glyphs move to two small fonts of their own that follow `carte_police_echelle` too (`mdi_font_temp`, 45 px on the Tab5; `mdi_font_pas`, 56 px): at 45 px on the 7B, the living-room value's « ° » ended 4 px before the greenhouse icon, and the − nearly filled its 40 px button.
- **The render robot touches the 7B's home screen at its real places** (`tools/rendu/vers_carte.py`): a home-screen point of the Tab5 is brought to the card's screen with the same formula, so the top buttons, the − / + tile and the bottom tiles are really touched; the navigation replacement of the second lot no longer fires on the home screen (kept as a safety net).
- `Tab5/user_entities.yaml`'s `meteo_cell_width`, `meteo_cell_height` and `meteo_cell_y` are no longer read (the tiles follow the board) and leave the template, which now holds only comments (`esphome config` accepts a `user_entities.yaml` without any key, checked on both configurations).
- On the Tab5 the resolved configuration is identical to the second lot's except for one build flag, `-DTAB5_POLICE_PCT=100` (the header's default).
- Not done here: each popup's own layout and the games (later lots).

## Fourth lot: each popup's own layout follows the card (2026-10-13)

Developed in parallel with the third lot (the home screen); games stay out of scope.

- **Same layout, smaller**: every literal of a popup's own layout is the Tab5's value brought to the body of the card in the expression itself, rounded down — a width or an x `${ 386 * (corps_w | int) // 1202 }`, a height or a y `${ 456 * (corps_h | int) // 598 }`, a square the smaller of the two, a negative `${ 0 - … }`. The C++ does the same with `largeur_carte()`, `hauteur_carte()` and `taille_carte()` (`tab5_geometrie.h`). On the Tab5 every ratio is 1: the resolved configuration is identical (`esphome config`, substitutions aside, checked after each family) and the C++ constants keep their values (asserted by the tests).
- **Families, one commit each**: Shutters and Lights, Climate, Settings and the system console, Temperature and Weather, Alarm clock, Energy, TV remote, Cameras, Plants, Voice assistant, Device. Each has a test that resolves its YAML and its C++ for every board, checks that both give the same numbers and that everything fits in the card, and pins the Tab5's values (`tests/test_*_popup.py`, `test_energie.py`, `test_historique.py`, `test_telecommandes.py`); `constantes_cpp()` (`tests/commun.py`) evaluates the C++ `constexpr` per board.
- **What does not shrink is placed from what does**: `roboto_22`, the MDI fonts and the weather fonts keep their size on every board; the bold Roboto sizes are scaled by `carte_police_echelle`. Line heights come from the font's metrics (`ligne_roboto_130_b`, `ligne_roboto_45_b`, `ligne_roboto_32_b`, tokens): the Alarm clock's rollers are as tall as their rows, and what sits under a roller is placed from its bottom.
- **Departures from "same layout, smaller"**, where a fixed-size text no longer fitted:
  - Plants: five cards of 178 pixels on the 7B; the metric rows' margin gives way (14 → 5 pixels) and their text moves from x 50 to 38 so that « Température » (128 pixels in `roboto_22`) fits beside its icon — only when the card is too narrow (`pot_ligne_marge`, `pot_texte_x`).
  - Cameras: the picture is asked from Home Assistant in eighths of 1920 × 1080 (HA's scaling steps), the largest that fits the body with the name, time and dots under it: 4/8 (960 × 540) on the Tab5, 3/8 (720 × 405) on the 7B (`cameras_huitiemes`, `kImageHuitiemes`); the mosaic's cells follow (356 × 198 on the 7B, thumbnails asked at 360 × 202 and scaled by `echelle_couvrir()`).
  - Voice assistant: the settings panel and the answer card end 8 pixels above the action bar (`corps_h - 8 -` its height); the « Your request » card keeps its 104 pixels (two lines of `roboto_22`).
  - TV remote: the bottom row keeps its 102 pixels; the pad is a square of the smaller ratio and the commands column takes what is left.
  - After a look at the 7B renders, a fixed-size word is placed from its fixed-size icon rather than scaled with the card (TV remote keys and On / Off, « Settings » of the voice assistant, the console's Flash value; « Marche / Arrêt », 142 pixels, then wraps onto two lines inside its key on the 7B, its width `SIZE_CONTENT` on the Tab5 as before), or shifted / dropped only when it would not fit: Boost / Eco icon and word move left together (0 on the Tab5, 17 pixels on the 7B), the Alarm clock's hint under the rollers loses its 22-pixel margin (one line on the 7B), the Cameras time keeps its 360 pixels for « Touch a picture to enlarge it », and a weather column of 61 pixels shows a rain amount without « mm » and the hour instead of « Tomorrow » when they do not fit (measured with `lv_text_get_size()`).
- Not verified when written: nothing ran on a Waveshare. The hardware JPEG decoder with 405-pixel-high pictures and the speed of the scaled thumbnails are unknown; the 120-pixel weather icon sits in a 119-pixel box on the 7B.

## On the board (2026-10-14)

The board arrived on 2026-10-14 and has run the firmware since (the author's daily screen). Two settings written before it came were wrong and are fixed in `carte-waveshare-7b.yaml` and `geometrie-waveshare-7b.yaml`: the panel is mounted upside down (`carte_rotation: "180"`, the GT911 mirrored in X and Y, its reset on GPIO 23, as in the public configurations that run on this board), and the ESP32-C6's reset is active high like the Tab5's. The PSRAM at `speed: 200MHz`, already in the board package, proved necessary: the first trial firmware, without it, had about 30 display underruns a second. The ESPHome floor of this board, 2026.9.1, is in the Consequences above. What happened on the board, in order, and what is not verified yet: [Waveshare ESP32-P4 7B](../waveshare-7b.md#problems-met-and-how-they-were-solved).

Since 2026-10-19 ([ADR-0069](0069-screen-direction-from-home-assistant.md)), a board mounted the other way round no longer needs a build with `carte_rotation: "0"`: its « Sens de l'écran » setting (« Retourné ») adds the 180° while running, and the published firmware fits both mountings.
