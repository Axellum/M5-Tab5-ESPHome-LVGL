# ADR-0040: In HA mode, the climate card shows the room on screen — its temperature, its humidity, and its climate on the − / + tile

**Status:** Proposed (2026-10-09, asked for by the author; not tried on a tablet when written).
**Date:** 2026-10-09

## Context

Since [ADR-0023](0023-rooms-generic-tiles.md) the bottom row shows one room at a time in HA mode, but the climate card above it kept the two fixed temperatures of the 3.x home page (living room on the left, greenhouse on the right) and the − / + tile its own choice ([ADR-0033](0033-adjustable-tile.md)). Swiping to the bedroom left the living-room temperature on screen. The author asked for « the temperature of the room (and its humidity, optional), and a climate per room, in place of the living-room and greenhouse temperatures ».

Constraints: push-only and events-only ([ADR-0001](0001-push-only-zero-polling.md), [ADR-0025](0025-events-only.md)); no new action variable (the API contract stays at 1.0.0, `contrat/contrat.yaml`); a firmware older than this one must keep working with the new blueprint, and the other way round; no hardcoded colour; every icon in its font's `glyphs:` (code rule 9).

## Decision

- **Three optional inputs per room** in the « Pièce n » sections of the blueprint: `piece_n_temperature` (sensor, temperature), `piece_n_humidite` (sensor, humidity), `piece_n_clim` (climate); empty by default. Only the temperature turns the feature on for a room.
- **Keys of `tab5_maj_emplacements`**, after the reglables, room by room: `crpR|min|max|step|unit|capabilities|name` (the room climate's settings, the fields of `crRT`, [ADR-0027](0027-climate-per-tile.md)), `pR|temperature|humidity|climate` (empty field: nothing declared; `nan`: unknown value; climate `1` or `0`), `cepR|setpoint|room|mode|preset|fan|swing` (its state, the fields of `ceRT`). Every room is pushed after a (re)connection, empty ones too (`pR|||0`: the tablet forgets what was removed); then a room when a sensor or its climate changed (slow measures, 5 minutes), or at once when its climate changes state or setpoint (two triggers per room, generated by `tools/gen_blueprint_emplacements.py`). Older firmware ignores these keys (unknown key).
- **The climate card follows the room in HA mode** (`tab5_piece_climat.cpp`, the only writer of `icon_salon`, `val_temp_salon`, `icon_serre`, `current_serre` since this ADR): on a room with a declared temperature, a thermometer and the room's temperature on the left (the icon tinted by the humidity when there is one), a drop and the humidity on the right, or nothing. In weather mode, or on a room without temperature, the screen of before: sofa, greenhouse (or the arcade pad without a greenhouse), last values received, the styles' colours.
- **The − / + tile adjusts the room's climate** when the room shown has one (`piece_climat_clim()`), ahead of the choice made in its list, which is kept and comes back out of HA mode. − / + move the setpoint (`clim_piece_pas()`, same debounce as a tile climate), a tap on the value opens the climate popup on it. Its commands go to the place `cpR` (`pR` + `eteindre` already means « Pièce : tout éteindre »); the blueprint sends them to that room's climate only, translated by the same « Clim : … » branches as every other climate.
- **A long press on the room's temperature opens its history** ([ADR-0032](0032-temperature-history-popup.md)) with the key `pR`, no forecast; the right side (humidity) has no history. The short tap keeps its behaviour (the − / + list).
- **Room climates share the PSRAM table of the tile climates**: 5 more cells after the 25 of the tiles.

## Rejected

- **A new API action** (`tab5_maj_piece_climat`): changes the public contract (a major version for the HA files that call it), while an unknown key of `tab5_maj_emplacements` is already ignored by older firmware.
- **Reusing the tile climates** (a `cli` tile of the room as its climate): a room's climate is often not on its tiles (five places), and the wish was « a climate per room », chosen once.
- **`pR` as the command place**: taken by « Pièce : tout éteindre » since ADR-0023.
- **The climate's own `current_temperature` as the room's temperature**: the author asked for a sensor of the room; a climate's sensor is often on the indoor unit, near the ceiling.

## Consequences

- Update order: either side alone changes nothing. New blueprint with an older firmware: keys ignored, screen of before. New firmware with an older blueprint: no `pR` key, screen of before. Both are needed for the feature.
- A room climate's settings (`crpR`) only leave with its state changes or a reconnection: a climate that changes its settings without changing its state is seen at the next reconnection, like the tile climates.
- The − / + list still opens in HA mode on a room with a climate, but a choice made there shows only out of HA mode or on another room. To revisit with the climate carousel (pull request #414, ADR-0038 there), not merged when this was written: its pages should list the room climates too.
- One more file (`tab5_piece_climat.*`); about 5 × 180 bytes more of PSRAM when a room climate is known; two icons (thermometer F050F, drop F058E) already in `mdi_font_45`.
